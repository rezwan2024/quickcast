But `recorder.ondataavailable` keeps firing every ~1s regardless, pushing new chunks onto that *same array* while the `await` was in flight; the reassignment silently discarded every chunk that arrived during the upload. `session.pendingBytes` kept counting them (it's incremented independently in `ondataavailable`), so it and the actual contents of `session.pendingBlobs` desynced — the next iteration's `uploadSize` (computed from the now-too-high `pendingBytes`) could exceed the real blob's size, and `Blob.slice()` silently clamps instead of erroring, so the chunk actually sent was smaller than what `session.uploadedBytes` was then incremented by.
